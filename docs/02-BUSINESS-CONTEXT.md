1. **End-User Customers (Vehicle Owners)**:
   - Visit the public site to discover products, verify authenticity, learn application specs, and locate certified installers.
   - Do not purchase coatings directly on the website (professional application required).
2. **Dealers (Detailing Studios, Detailers & Certified Workshops)**:
   - Apply for dealership through public lead funnels.
   - Access the dedicated **Dealer Portal** for catalog assets, technical application guides, certifications, and wholesale replenishment.
3. **Distributors (Regional Supply Partners)**:
   - Manage regional inventory, fulfill bulk orders to dealers, and coordinate hub shipments.
   - Operate inside the private portal.
4. **Trionyx Management (Managing Director / Super Admins)**:
   - Oversee company operations, approve dealer applications, review regional metrics, and manage catalog data via the private administrative portal.

## 3. Platform Division of Surfaces

### 1. Public Website
- **Audience**: Vehicle owners, car enthusiasts, prospective dealers, industry partners.
- **Content**: Brand narrative, verified history, chemical technology, product specifications, customer reviews, network coverage, and contact channels.
- **Goal**: Authority, brand demand, and dealer inquiry capture.

### 2. Dealer Portal
- **Audience**: Verified, approved certified detailing studios and installers.
- **Content**: Dealer dashboard, wholesale ordering, marketing collaterals, warranty registration, product application bulletin downloads.
- **Access**: Secure authenticated login (`/dealer/login`).

### 3. Private Internal Operations Portal
- **Audience**: Regional distributors, Managing Director, executive staff, administrative users.
- **Content**: Regional fulfillment, distributor stock, dealer verification pipeline, customer review moderation, administrative management.
- **Access**: Restricted internal authentication (`/portal/login`). Strictly isolated from public pages.
