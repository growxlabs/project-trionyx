## 1. Canonical Positioning

> **Trionyx is an India-focused automotive products brand established in 2006. It operates across automotive protection, coatings, care and related product categories, supported by dealer and distributor relationships.**

---

## 2. What Trionyx IS and IS NOT

### Trionyx IS:
- An **automotive products brand** with physical formulations, coatings, films, and accessories.
- An **established entity** operating since **2006** (~20 years in the Indian automotive market).
- A **B2B-enabled brand** whose primary go-to-market runs through certified installers, detailers, and distribution networks across India.
- A **product-led company** focused on material performance, chemical durability, surface protection, and vehicle endurance.

### Trionyx is NOT:
- ❌ **NOT a detailing studio**: Trionyx does not operate detailing garages or schedule car wash appointments.
- ❌ **NOT a car dealership**: Trionyx does not sell, trade, or service automobiles.
- ❌ **NOT a generic e-commerce store**: No shopping carts, "add to cart" buttons, flash sales, discount coupons, or consumer checkout funnels.
- ❌ **NOT only a ceramic-coating company**: Ceramic coating is one product line among many (graphene, borophene, PPF, lighting, care products, accessories).
- ❌ **NOT a software/SaaS company**: No software subscriptions, cloud dashboards for consumers, or tech-startup jargon.

---

## 3. What the Website Represents

The website represents the **official Trionyx brand and product portfolio**. It functions as:
1. The **definitive public brand identity** for vehicle owners and studio owners.
2. The **technical catalog** showcasing formulations, material science, and protection standards.
3. The **acquisition channel** for certified installers, studios, and authorized regional dealers.
4. The **entry point for Dealer Access** and authorized partner workflows.
5. The gateway to **private internal distributor and management operations**, kept strictly separated from the public presentation.

---

## 4. Primary Business & Platform Goals

```text
1. Build Brand Credibility: Establish 20 years of experience (Est. 2006) through calm, restrained, high-end editorial authority.
2. Present Products Clearly: Showcase formulations, specs, benefits, and applications without consumer retail clutter.
3. Support Dealer Acquisition: Convert high-caliber detailing studios and automotive businesses into certified dealers and partners.
4. Deliver a Premium Brand Experience: Provide vehicle owners with confident, precision-engineered reassurance.
5. Provide Dealer Access: Enable authorized dealers to access product resources, documentation, and ordering workflows.
6. Support Private Operations: Facilitate secure distributor, management, and administrative functions in isolated private surfaces.
```
