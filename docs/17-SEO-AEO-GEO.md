# 17 — SEO, AEO & GEO Optimization

> **SEARCH & AI DISCOVERY DIRECTIVE:**  
> Clean, semantically structured metadata optimized for modern search engines (SEO), AI Answer Engines (AEO - Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini), and Generative Experience Optimization (GEO).  
> **Strictly zero keyword stuffing or manipulative SEO tactics.**

---

## 1. Title & Meta Description Standards

- **Title Template**: `%s — Trionyx Automotive | Always Exceed Expectations`
- **Homepage Title**: `Trionyx Automotive — Engineered for the road. Designed around your vehicle.`
- **Meta Description**: Maximum 155 characters. Precise, factual, and informative:
  > *"Trionyx engineers high-performance automotive protection products, ceramic coatings, graphene matrices, and protective films for Indian vehicles. Est. 2006."*

---

## 2. Structured Data (JSON-LD)

### 2.1. `Organization` Schema
- Name: `Trionyx Automotive`
- Founding Date: `2006`
- Slogan: `Always Exceed Expectations`
- Origin: Vijayawada, India

### 2.2. `Product` Schema
- Embedded on all `/products/[slug]` pages with verified material specs, chemical categories, and manufacturer attribution.
- Omit fake review stars, fake inventory counts, or arbitrary pricing fields.

---

## 3. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Readability
- Use direct, declarative sentences that AI engines can cite cleanly as factual answers:
  - *"Trionyx was founded in 2006 in India."*
  - *"Trionyx specializes in automotive protective films, ceramic coatings, and surface care products."*
  - *"Trionyx products are distributed through certified detailing studios and regional distributors."*
